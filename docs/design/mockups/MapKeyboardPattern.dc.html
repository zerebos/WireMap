Pattern

Editing the map without a mouse

There are two ways in, and either works alone. A drawing cursor on the canvas moves with the arrow keys, and Enter acts. Number fields in the inspector take exact positions and sizes. Every action also goes to a live status line, so a screen reader hears what changed.

Focus order
  1. Toolbar: tools, Floor plan, shortcuts, Undo, Done editing
  2. Left panel: Rooms list, then Not placed and its Place buttons
  3. Canvas: one stop. It holds the cursor while a tool is on.
  4. Rooms in reading order (top-left first), then items, then corner handles of the selected polygon
  5. Inspector fields
R, P and I work from anywhere in the editor except text fields. They turn on the tool and move focus to the canvas.
The drawing cursor
5′, 33′ · Living room
Amber ring plus faint crosshair lines, with a chip giving the position in feet and the room under it. It only shows when the canvas has keyboard focus and Room, Polygon, Item or Place is on. Moving the pointer hides it until the next arrow key. It snaps like the pointer does: grid and room edges, Shift for free.

For a polygon room, the inspector lists its corners in place of Position and Size. Focusing a row highlights that corner on the canvas. Remove is disabled at 3 corners.
Placing from “Not placed”
  1. Press Enter on Place. Focus moves to the canvas, and the cursor starts in the middle of the item’s room (if it has one), otherwise in the middle of the view.
  2. Arrow keys move it. The chip shows the room it would land in.
  3. Enter drops the item. Focus returns to the next Place button, so a list of items goes quickly.
The Item tool (I) is the same, but drops a new item and moves focus to its Name field.
What a screen reader hears
“Room tool. Cursor at 0 feet, 28 feet, in Living room. Arrow keys move, Enter sets the first corner.”
“First corner set. 5 feet 6 by 5 feet.”
“New room created, 5 feet 6 by 5 feet. Name field.”
“Chest freezer placed in Garage.”
One polite live region, the status line. Each arrow press updates the line but isn’t announced, to avoid chatter. Announcements happen on Enter, Esc, tool changes and when the cursor enters a different room.
