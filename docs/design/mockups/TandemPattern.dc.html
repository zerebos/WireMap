Tandem breakers

Two half-width breakers in one space. Each half is its own breaker with its own label, amps and items. Both halves sit on the same leg. Everywhere a breaker is named, a tandem half is “17A” or “17B”.

Naming & position
17A
17B
A = upper half, B = lower half.Position text: “Left, row 9 · lower half”.Header counts spaces and breakers separately: “29 of 40 spaces · 31 breakers”.
Some brands print their own numbers on tandems. If the directory says “17, 18” for a tandem, the user can type that into the label; the slot stays 17A/17B.
